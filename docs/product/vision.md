# Product Vision — iC.edu Assessment Platform (IAP)

## Vision Statement
To be the premier, enterprise-grade online Computer-Based Testing (CBT), Learning Management, and Credential Verification Platform that empowers educational institutions, certification bodies, and corporate organizations to deliver secure, automated, and scalable assessments worldwide.

## Strategic Objectives
1. **End-to-End Evaluation & Credentialing**: Provide a frictionless pipeline from product discovery, course enrollment, CBT delivery, automated scoring, to digital certificate verification.
2. **Modular & Extensible Architecture**: Maintain clean domain separation (Academic, QuestionBank, Assessment, Certificate, Commerce, Reporting) so new evaluation types (TOEFL, TOEIC, IELTS, Placement Tests) can be plugged in seamlessly.
3. **Enterprise Reliability & Security**: Guarantee deterministic test delivery, server-time countdown truth, anti-cheating audit trails, and cryptographically verifiable digital credentials.
